## The Hierarchy

```
Narrative
  └── Thread
        └── Sub-thread
              └── Block
                    └── Chunk
```

| Level | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| **Narrative** | The full arc — the product story being told |
| **Thread** | A major strand of work within the narrative |
| **Sub-thread** | A focused branch within a thread |
| **Block** | A collaboration unit — multiple chunks working together with shared context |
| **Chunk** | Atomic work unit — assigned to one worker |

The "within" axis of 3D coordination (cross-functional teamwork) operates at every level — not just one team at one layer.
