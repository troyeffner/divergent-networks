Thread = individual JTBD that crosses surfaces — distributed, not confined to one screen. Touchpoint = individual contact point where a thread surfaces on a specific screen/surface. Task = what happens at a touchpoint. JTBD mapping: Narrative = Job Grouping (the research question that encompasses related jobs), Thread = Individual Job (the specific JTBD). NDD absorbs JTBD structure — no separate framework needed. The fractal quality: the same thread appears at multiple surfaces via different touchpoints. This enables: tracing a job across the whole product, finding gaps (threads with missing touchpoints), identifying hub screens (surfaces serving many threads), prioritizing builds (which touchpoint unlocks the most value). "Feature" replaced entirely. Level discipline: Narrative = research story, Thread = job, Touchpoint = contact surface, Task = action. Don't mix levels. Maps to Workflow Builder: Thread = WB thread, Touchpoint = WB subthread, Task = WB task. Also produces OOUX-compatible object model as output — {OOUX} is an NDD narrative, not a borrowed framework. Full model captured in dispatch_20260304_ndd_fractal_object_model.md. |
